There's more functions that can be abused, but you're on your own if you want to mess with these.

FactoryReset - 0 args
SetLockdownMode - 1 arg, boolean
DeleteAccount - 0 args
AddSplitTunnelApp - 1 arg, string(?)
SetAllowLan - 1 arg, boolean

GetDevice - 0 args, returns info of your device (device uuid, device name, account number, port ranges, etc)
GetAccountData - 0 args, returns info of when your subscription runs out/expires, whether its active, etc
